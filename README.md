#### 🔬 Current research
**Aerosols vs. lightning over tropical Africa (M.Sc. thesis)**
I study the *"boomerang effect"*: lightning first increases with aerosol loading, then drops off at high loading. The study covers the Sahel and Central African biomass-burning belt and uses the first geostationary lightning imager over Africa (**MTG Lightning Imager**). Supporting data:
- Aerosol optical depth: iAERUS-GEO and CAMS reanalysis
- Fire radiative power
- Land-surface temperature
- Precipitation: GPM IMERG
- Cloud-top temperature: MTG FCI
- **EarthCARE** space lidar profiles
